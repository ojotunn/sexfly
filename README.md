# SEX FLY

Two whole fruit-fly brains (FlyWire connectome, 138,639 neurons each, LIF model of Shiu et al. 2024) simulated live on one
GPU, drawn as two neuron clouds, with two 3D flies below: she walks by what her neurons say; he courts her and mounts her.
Every trade of the token on Pons (Robinhood Chain) is a stimulus for BOTH brains. Buys raise the couple's libido: he sings
with one wing (her Johnston's organ hears it), then she lets him mount and her pC1 receptivity neurons fire; the rhythm
follows the libido and his own walking drive. Sells cool it. A big sell fires her DNp13 rejection neurons: she kicks him off.

Derived from the FLY BRAIN project (same engine, body, relay). Processes: `brain/servidor.py` twice (FLY_QUEM=ela port 8435,
FLY_QUEM=ele port 8436), `corpo/corpo.py` (her body), `mercado/mercado.py` (market → stimuli for both, libido meter,
her orders on other Pons curves). `START-Windows.bat` starts all four. Relay (`relay/servidor.py`, Railway) accepts two
sources (`/fonte?token=&quem=ela|ele`) and serves the public page.
